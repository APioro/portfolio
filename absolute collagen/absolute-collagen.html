Absolute Collagen
UX, E-commerce
As part of a university-led collaboration with Absolute Collagen, I was tasked with redesigning their mobile webshop to improve usability and visual coherence. The client needed simplified navigation, a clearer flow of product information, and a refreshed  UI. The unstructured product page inconsistent branding led to user drop-off.
To create a clean UI aligned with the brand’s modern tone I unified the color palette and limited gradients use. A clear typographic hierarchy was introduced to guide users more intuitively through content.
